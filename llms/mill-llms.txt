# Mill

> Mill makes a kitchen bin that dries and grinds food scraps into dry, nutrient-rich Food Grounds — keeping food waste out of landfills and the methane it produces out of the atmosphere. Customers fill the bin through the week, and Mill helps the grounds get reused rather than thrown away.
